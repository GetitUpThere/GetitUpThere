FAQ

The practical questions.

Do I own the website?

You receive a copy of the finished website files. There is no requirement to keep paying GetitUpThere simply to keep possession of the site.

Do I have to pay every month?

No compulsory monthly website or maintenance subscription is included in the standard service. A custom domain normally has its own annual renewal fee.

Can I use my existing domain?

Yes. If you already own a domain, it can normally be connected to the finished static site.

What if I don't have a domain?

You can register one in your own name. Guidance can be provided so the domain belongs to you rather than being locked to a developer.

Will the site work on phones?

Yes. The standard site is designed responsively for current mobile, tablet and desktop browsers.

Can you link my Google and Facebook pages?

Yes. Your website can link directly to your existing social profiles, Google Maps listing, phone number and other public business channels.

Can I add more later?

Yes. Extra pages, new sections and additional features can be quoted separately if your needs grow.

Is this right for every business?

No. Businesses needing e-commerce, complex bookings, membership systems or substantial web applications should use a more advanced solution.
